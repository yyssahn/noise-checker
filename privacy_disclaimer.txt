PRIVACY DISCLAIMER — Automated Noise Monitoring System

This system records ambient sound levels for the sole purpose of
documenting environmental noise (e.g. construction, traffic, impact
sounds) during overnight hours.

FREQUENCY FILTERING
A band-stop (notch) filter targeting 500 Hz – 2,500 Hz is intended to
be applied during recording. This range covers the fundamental
frequencies of human speech, significantly reducing the intelligibility
of any captured voices. The system is not designed or intended to
record, store, or transmit recognizable speech.

DATA HANDLING
- Recordings are compressed to 32 kbps mono MP3, further reducing
  audio fidelity.
- Files are automatically deleted after 90 days.
- Only aggregate decibel levels and timestamps are used for analysis.

This system is operated for personal, lawful use in documenting noise
conditions within the operator's own residence.
